# SpinStrike v0.5.0 economy report: rewards with tactical combat

Generated by `npm run economy:v05` (`scripts/economy-v05.ts`).

**Method**
- Every battle is a fresh Bronze III career with an unupgraded starter (all four starters).
- The player's launch timing is human-like (10 % Poor / 30 % Good / 40 % Great / 20 % Perfect).
- The player's abilities are either not used, or driven by the tactical AI at Normal or Hard skill, as a stand-in for people of different skill.
- The opponent is the career AI at the battle's difficulty. That difficulty sets its launch quality and aim and its tactical skill.
- Upgrade costs, the XP curve and the base rewards are unchanged from v0.4.1 ([v0.4.1 report](v0.4.1-economy-report.md)), so the long-term progression times there still apply, scaled by the win rates below.

Bronze III, unupgraded starters (all four), 150 battles per starter per cell = 600 per cell, seed 700001. Time per battle = simulated duration + 25 s overhead. Reward multipliers: Easy 0.75×, Normal 1×, Hard 1.1×, Expert 1.2×.

## Findings
- **Win rate falls with difficulty**, as intended. Against fresh Bronze III opponents (which carry upgrades), the win rate is about 50 % on Easy, 43–45 % on Normal, 38–42 % on Hard and 33–36 % on Expert.
- **Credits/hour and XP/hour are roughly flat from Normal to Expert** (±6 %). Easy pays about 13 % less.
  - The temporary multipliers (0.75 / 1.0 / 1.1 / 1.2) almost exactly offset the harder AI.
  - No difficulty is an exploit, and Expert is not a faster route to upgrades.
- **Using abilities well pays a little.** Hard-skill ability use earns 4–8 % more per hour than not using abilities on Normal and Expert. Normal-skill use is about break-even, because careless boosts cost stamina.

## Recommendation
- **Keep 0.75 / 1.0 / 1.1 / 1.2.** The evidence does not clearly call for a change.
- **Optional, needs approval:** if Expert should pay noticeably more per hour than Hard, 1.3× would add about 8 % to Expert Credits/hour.
