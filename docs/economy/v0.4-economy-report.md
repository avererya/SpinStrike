# SpinStrike v0.4 economy and progression report

Generated by `scripts/economy.ts`. **Part A is implemented progression** (real combat engine, Bronze opponents only). **Part B is a projection** of future ranks and is *not* in the game yet.

Time model: battle time = measured average simulated battle duration + 25 s overhead (menus, launch minigame, finish presentation, result screen). The overhead is an assumption.

Player model: human-like launch quality (10 % Poor / 30 % Good / 40 % Great / 20 % Perfect); upgrade policy: after every battle buy upgrades on the lowest-ranked affordable attribute (an even spread).

## Approved numbers used

- Full Common upgrade: 83,400 Credits (13,900 per attribute x 6).
- XP to level 30: 13,050; XP needed for each level-gate: L5 550, L10 1800, L15 3675, L20 6175, L25 9300.
- Bronze victory (Normal): 100 Credits + 50 XP. 500 starting Credits.

### Expert reward check

Expert pays 2x Credits and XP but, today, only improves the opponent's *launch* quality (Perfect 50 %, never Poor). Measured Inferno career win rates are in Part A. Expert reaches a fully upgraded top in 4.9 h (306) versus 7.3 h (450) on Normal. If Expert win rate is close to Normal's, the 2x multiplier is a free doubling of income; see the report recommendation.

## Bottleneck: XP or Credits?

At Bronze/Normal victory rates, level 30 needs 261 wins of XP, while maximising the six attributes needs 834 wins of Credits (before defeat/draw income and starting Credits). **Credits are the primary bottleneck** (about 3.2x XP). Level gates only briefly delay purchases: rank 10 needs level 25 (9,300 XP, 186 wins), long before the Credits for the last ranks are earned.
